report-gen brings findings, evidence and remediation into one editable project, then produces files a recipient can review.

From a finding to a reviewable delivery

  1. Document. Give the finding a stable identity, describe what was observed and attach evidence with its context and limits.
  2. Review. Inspect references, metadata and the produced files. Record editorial observations separately from remediation and retest status.
  3. Deliver. Produce PDF, DOCX, Markdown or portable HTML. Recipient-specific profiles select actual content; simply hiding part of a complete HTML report does not remove that data.

The editor runs locally. The browser exercise below is a small illustration of audience selection, not the editor itself.

Guidance without inventing results

Version {{REPORT_VERSION}} provides a guided or complete workspace and a curated library of 90 finding cards, including 30 about AI, LLMs and agents. Work packs begin with pending checks and unverified drafts. Adapt each card to the observed behaviour and attach your own evidence.

The interface, help and report output support Spanish, English and Brazilian Portuguese. Interface, library and report languages are separate choices; changing a language does not translate content you have written.

Compare evidence and track each affected instance

Import HTTP exchanges or HAR files with provenance and compare selected requests and responses. Differences are evidence to interpret; they do not establish a vulnerability or a successful correction. Keep assets, affected instances, remediation and retests connected so a partial correction does not close outstanding targets.

Turn findings into remediation work

Review changes to owner, due date and root cause for selected findings before applying them together. Preserve each finding’s identity, evidence and retests. Prepare reviewed packages by owner with readable findings and a CSV from the same saved revision. Track issuance, receipt and accepted changes, then accept returned fields individually. A reported correction never becomes a verified retest automatically; exchanging the files remains your decision.

Organise and protect your projects

  • Project catalogue. Search by title or client, sort by modification, due date or pending retests, and archive or reactivate projects without deleting their data.
  • Explicit recovery. Restore supported capture and decision forms in the same browser; recovery never attaches evidence or confirms publication automatically.
  • Reviewed imports. Nuclei JSONL creates unverified findings. Nmap XML adds assets and service observations to pending coverage; an open port is not a vulnerability.
  • Private backups. Encrypt saved projects with a password. Pending browser copies remain separate and must be saved into the project before moving devices.
  • Optional connections. Jira Cloud and DefectDojo exchanges require your configuration and review. Local editorial assistance with Ollama is optional. These connections do not verify findings or retests automatically.
  • Optional local OCR. Request a scan of an image for possible sensitive data. Tesseract runs locally; alerts require manual review and do not redact the image.

Use the full local application

On Linux or Kali, extract the source and run python3 scripts/install.py --desktop --lang en. Open report-gen from your application menu or run ~/.local/bin/report-gen, then visit http://127.0.0.1:8080 in your browser. Python 3.11+ and the documented dependencies are required. Keep the extracted application and its .venv; the launcher is not a standalone executable. Follow the guide for Windows and macOS.

The installer keeps data in $XDG_DATA_HOME/report-gen, or ~/.local/share/report-gen by default. Choose a different directory with --workspace and follow the guide to migrate existing projects.

“Update report-gen” checks the official stable Release only when you request it. Review the package before preparing a verified download and private backup in a new directory. Stop the current editor, then follow the installation and recovery instructions. Preparation never executes downloaded code; browser drafts require separate saving or downloading.

For support, open “Check installation”, review the synthetic diagnostic report and download it. The JSON includes version, generic operating system and test results; it excludes projects, paths and usernames. Review final exports in the recipient’s software; structural DOCX checks do not replace opening the document in Word.
